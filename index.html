Free Online Handwriting Animation Tool — No Software, No Coding

Textmover is a free online animation tool that lets you create stunning handwriting, chalkboard, typewriter, and console typing animations directly in your browser. No software download or installation required — and absolutely no coding experience needed. Simply type your text, choose a style, and generate professional animated videos in minutes.

Whether you need animated handwriting for social media, educational content, business presentations, or creative projects, Textmover makes it easy for anyone to produce realistic text animations for free. Works on any device with a web browser — no plugins, no sign-up fees, no technical skills required.

Simply login to the app, generate the animation and record using your computer or device's screen record and embed anywhere you wish.

You can also use green screen or other plain backgrounds to embed your animation in or on top of other content using your favorite video editors. Alternatively after trying the software, contact us for a HTML or SVG or similar custom application, that suits your animation needs for a reasonable price which will run in your browser.
